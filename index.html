Quick Reference Guide

  1. Choose your forms — check the forms you need (816, 817, and/or 819); your BTH email says which ones.
  2. Enter your name and school.
  3. Set the month — it defaults to the current month; use the start/end day options only for a partial month.
  4. Add your classes — type each class code in order; the times fill in automatically.
  5. Generate — your completed PDFs download automatically.
  6. Open each PDF in a reader that lets you fill and sign (such as Adobe Acrobat Reader or Mac Preview), add your signature and date, and submit to BTH as usual.
