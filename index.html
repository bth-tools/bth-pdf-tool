Quick Reference Guide

  1. Choose your appearance — use System, Light, or Dark at the top. This changes the screen only; your PDFs keep their original appearance.
  2. Pick your forms — check the ones you need (816, 817, and/or 819); your BTH email says which.
  3. Import a STAR schedule (optional) — in MyUH Services, open STAR GPS Registration, choose the current term, and Print → Save as PDF. Choose that file under Import a STAR schedule. Compare every course, credit, meeting time and date with the original, including whether any courses were missed. Check any extraction notices, then select Use these classes. This replaces your class list; you can still edit everything before generating. Scanned pages use local text recognition and need extra care.
  4. Type your name and school. Or review the name and school filled by the schedule import above.
  5. Pick the month — it starts on this month. First-To-Work reports whole Sunday–Saturday weeks, and a week counts for the month its Friday falls in, so a form can open or close in the next-door month: September 2026 runs Su 8/30 to Sa 9/26. The dropdown shows each month’s span, and the start/end dates start on the full period — change them only for part of a period.
  6. Add unavailable times if needed — select the weekdays and start/end times for work or other commitments. Automatic async attendance, additional class hours, and study avoid these blocks. Fixed class meetings stay as entered; conflicts are flagged.
  7. Add your classes — type each class code in order; the times fill in for you. Leave Credits on 3 unless a class is worth a different number. If imported, review the populated classes instead. Expand Course dates or a meeting's date options to review date limits. These limits stay in place when you change the report month. Study times are calculated by the tool, not extracted from STAR.
  8. Set class times if needed — turn on “Does this class meet in person or online at scheduled times?” and enter its days and times. Leave it off for async classes. Expand Automatic scheduling to change the default 8:00 a.m. start for automatic attendance and study times.
  9. If a class meets for fewer hours than its credits, its row says so and choose Include additional approved class hours when applicable. For online activities counted as class time, such as recorded lectures.
  10. Choose study times only if needed — turn on the option under a class and choose a day and start time. One slot gets the full weekly study allowance. Add another slot to split it evenly, or enter hours for a specific slot. End times are calculated. These are preferred times: conflicts or insufficient space move study to another opening in the same week. Review the actual study schedule shown under the class.
  11. Check the hours summary — it shows exactly what the forms will document for the selected dates before you generate them. Study is limited to recorded attendance per class and FTW week, including additional class hours selected. UH observed holidays are skipped for set-time attendance. Async attendance and all study can use holidays. If hours cannot fit, the notice lists the unplaced hours; you can still generate the forms. Spring break, other non-instructional days, and one-off closures are not filtered.
  12. Generate — your finished PDFs download. If a month needs more rows than one form holds, you also get a file ending “_continued” — submit that one too.
  13. Open each PDF in a reader that lets you sign (like Adobe Acrobat Reader or Mac Preview), sign it, and send it to BTH as usual.
